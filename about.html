About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Story

Riverbend Yoga Studio was founded to give our community a peaceful place to move, breathe, and slow down. We believe yoga should be for everyone, no matter their age, size, or experience.

Meet Our Instructor

Our lead instructor has years of experience teaching beginner and intermediate classes. Her classes focus on good form, steady breathing, and having fun.

Our Studio

Our studio has large windows with views of the hills, warm wooden floors, and plenty of room to stretch out.
